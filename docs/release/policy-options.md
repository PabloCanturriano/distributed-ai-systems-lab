## License comparison

| Candidate       | Permissions and obligations in the published text                                                                                                                                                                          | Decision point                                                                        |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| MIT             | Allows use, modification and redistribution, including commercial use, while retaining its copyright and permission notice. See the [OSI license text](https://opensource.org/license/mit).                                | Prefer a short permission/notice policy when that matches the owner's reuse goals.    |
| Apache-2.0      | Includes an explicit contributor patent grant and termination provision, requires license/notice retention and notices of modified files. See [sections 2–4 of the ASF text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). | Evaluate explicit patent terms and the additional redistribution/notice requirements. |
| Defer selection | The repository has no selected license. Keep the public release decision pending while deciding the intended audience, ownership and redistribution policy.                                                                | Do not label the project open source or add a license identifier before selection.    |

The owner should decide whether external reuse is intended, who owns contributions, and whether the explicit patent terms are desired. Dependency licenses and notices must also be inventoried for the actual images; choosing a source license does not complete that inventory.
